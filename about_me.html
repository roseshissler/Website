Biography

My name is Rose Shissler! I am a Pratt graduate student, pursuing an MS in Museums and Digital Culture. I love information and exploring how I can promote, preserve and protect it!

Skill Objectives

  1. Promote and secure intellectual freedom in cultural heritage spaces.
  2. Create opportunities to evaluate and adjust policy to better represent communities, creators, and cultures.
  3. Lead institutions in advancing tools to protect, sustain, and grow their collections.
  4. Develop policy that advances sustainability, builds more equitable institutions, and protects cultural heritage for future generations.
  5. Connect community members to each other.
